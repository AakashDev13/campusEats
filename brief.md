# CampusEats Brief

## What

CampusEats is a campus food ordering system that connects students with campus food vendors. Students can browse available food, place orders, make payments, and track their orders. Vendors can manage their menus and process incoming orders.

## Who

The main users of CampusEats are:

- Students
- Food vendors
- Campus administrators
- Delivery staff

## Nouns

The main nouns/entities in the system are:

- Student
- Vendor
- Restaurant
- Menu
- Food Item
- Cart
- Order
- Order Item
- Payment
- Delivery
- Review

- Order
- Pay

- Track
- Review
- Update
- Manage

## Example Interactions

- A student browses a vendor's menu.
- A student adds food items to a cart.
- A student places an order.
- A student makes a payment.
- A student tracks an order.
- A vendor updates menu items.
- A vendor accepts and manages orders.
- An administrator manages users and vendors.
